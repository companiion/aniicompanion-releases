See where you are in the Egg Heist Lost Sanctum

A map on top of the game shows you, the nests and your route.

  1. You. The yellow arrow follows your character.
  2. Nest. The gold ring shows the chance that a nest is there.
  3. Route. The blue line leads to the nests you have not checked yet.

The app only looks at the image of the game window, like a screenshot. It does not read memory, inject code or use mods. Aniimo has an anti-cheat and nobody can promise anything: using it is your decision. Learn more

What it shows

What shows up on the map

You on the map, live

The app finds you on the game minimap and draws the whole sanctum on top of your screen.

  • Full screen or windowThe window is small: you can move and resize it.
  • Turns with you or stays stillIt turns with the camera, or stays north up.
  • Hides and returns by itselfWith the bag, a chest or a menu open, it hides.
See the map

The chance of each nest

Each spot shows the chance of a nest in this match.

  • Ring and numberGold ring and ≈45% beside it: the chance of a nest being there.
  • Green ✓The nest was already searched, by you or by a friend.
  • “boss 40%” badgeThe chance of the boss being in that room.
See the nests

Route through the nests

A blue line leads to the nests still missing. Thirty seconds before each monster wave, a card at the top of the screen counts down.

Questions

Quick questions

Does it work on my PC?

It was tested on 64-bit Windows 11. Windows 10 should work but has not been tested. The game must be windowed or borderless fullscreen. See what you need

Will I get banned?

The app only looks at the image of the game window, like a screenshot. Aniimo has an anti-cheat and we do not speak for the developers. Nobody can promise that a tool will never be flagged. Using it is your choice and at your own risk. Details

Is it free?

Yes. It is free, with no account and no data collection. It is a fan project, not affiliated with Pawprint Studio / FunPlus.

The map does not show up. Now what?

Check that the game is windowed or borderless fullscreen and that you are already inside the sanctum. See Troubleshooting
