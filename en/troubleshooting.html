Installing

The app will not open

The app opened in the wrong language

Click the globe button at the top of the app window and pick Português or English. The screens reload in the new language; your match, the map and your friends stay as they are.

Windows says “protected your PC”

Click More info, then Run anyway.

The warning shows up because the app is free and has no paid code-signing certificate. You normally only see it the first time. See it with pictures.

My antivirus removed the app

Open Windows Security, then Virus & threat protection and Protection history. Find AniiCompanion and click Actions and Restore.

If it keeps happening, go to Manage settings and Exclusions, and add the AniiCompanion folder. An exclusion applies to that folder only, so keep other files out of it.

Smart App Control blocked it (Windows 11)

That feature opens no unsigned program, and it has no button to allow just one. The app does not work with it turned on.

Turning it off is your decision. Once it is off, Windows will only let you turn it back on if you reinstall Windows.

The map

The map looks wrong

The map does not show up

Check these four things.

Is Aniimo in windowed or borderless fullscreen mode? Exclusive fullscreen cannot be captured.

Are you inside the Lost Sanctum of Egg Heist? The map only shows up in there.

Did you press F1? It shows and hides the map.

Did you open the bag, a chest or a menu? The map hides on purpose and comes back by itself.

The map is zoomed in, cut off or stuck

Press Ctrl + Shift + F1, or click Restart map at the top of the app window.

Your match is not lost: nests, eggs and chests stay marked.

It says “Looking for you”

The app lost your position inside the sanctum. Your arrow turns into a gray dot with a “?” showing where you were.

If it takes long to come back, use Restart map (Ctrl + Shift + F1). If it always happens in the same place, save a report.

The F1 key does not work

While AniiCompanion is open, F1, Ctrl + F1 and Ctrl + Shift + F1 belong to it.

If another program already uses F1 (for example, another Aniimo map), close that program first.

Files

Where settings live

Where does the app keep settings and logs?

In the %LOCALAPPDATA%\AniimoCompanion folder. Paste that address into the File Explorer bar to open it.

To remove everything, delete the app folder and this one too. How to uninstall.

Party

A friend does not show up right

My friend does not show up on the map

Only people in the party show up. After adding your friend, use Invite in the Friends tab.

Check that the 3 pictures are the same in both apps. If they differ, click They differ: someone may be in the middle.

My friend’s dot turned gray

Gray means more than 15 seconds without a position: the map shows where they were last seen.

An arrow on the edge of the screen means they are outside the part you can see.

I see my friend, but not their nests and eggs

Both of you need version 0.2.8 or newer to share nests and eggs. Update both apps.

Updating

The new version did not arrive

The app did not update

It checks when it opens and every 6 hours. When there is a new version, New version … · Update shows at the top of its window. Click it, read the news and choose Update now.

If it does not show up, download the new version by hand. How to update · Which file to download.

I want to go back to the previous version

When it updates, the app keeps the old version in a folder called AniiCompanion.anterior, next to the app folder.

Close AniiCompanion and open the AniiCompanion.exe inside it. Your settings, friends and downloaded maps are kept.

Report

I need to ask for help

How do I send a report?

In the app window, click Save report, at the bottom. The app version shows under it: tell us which one.

A .zip file goes to your Downloads folder, which the app opens for you.

Send that file to whoever gave you the app.

What is inside the report?

The app logs, a few screenshots of the game window taken at important moments, and the match data the app kept (summary and screen pictures with names blacked out). The logs may contain your nickname, read from the screen. Other windows open at the time may show up in them: look before sending.

The button sends nothing, it only creates the file. You decide whether to send it, and to whom.

Nothing worked?

Make a report and send it to whoever gave you the app. Wondering what the app does with your PC? See Privacy.
